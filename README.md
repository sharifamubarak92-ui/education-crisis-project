# 📚 The Power of Education in Times of Crisis

> Analyzing the Relationship Between Education and Income During Global Crises (1994–2023)

## 📖 Overview
This project analyzes how education influences income stability during crisis periods, based on U.S. data covering **17 million records** of full-time workers from 1994 to 2023.

## 🎯 Study Goals
- Understand the relationship between education level and income.
- Measure income resilience during crises (2000, 2008, 2020).
- Identify professions with the highest return on education.

## 📊 Key Findings
| Metric | Percentage |
|--------|-----------|
| Income improvement opportunity through education | **13.5×** |
| Highest bachelor's degree holders (2023) | 22.60% |
| Secondary education holders (2023) | 4.56% |
